EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This agreement is between ABC Technologies Pvt Ltd
and employee John Smith.

1. The employee will work as a Software Developer.
2. The employment period is 2 years.
3. The employee will receive a salary of Rs. 5,00,000 per year.
4. The employee must not work for any competing company
   for 3 years after leaving the organization.
5. The company can terminate employment without prior notice.
6. The employee must maintain confidentiality.
7. Any disputes will be handled only in the company's
   registered city.

Signed by both parties.
